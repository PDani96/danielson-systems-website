About

Danielson Systems

Danielson Systems is the independent industrial controls and systems integration practice of Preston Danielson, based in The Colony, Texas. I founded it in 2026 to take on contract work across the full lifecycle of a control system — electrical design, PLC and HMI programming, the software that runs alongside the hardware, and the commissioning that puts it into production — drawing on ten-plus years across industrial automation, energy storage, material handling, aerospace, and HVAC.

My background runs in two parallel tracks. On the hardware side: drafting schematics, designing panels, and programming PLCs for full system integration with drives, sensors, and safety devices. On the software side: industrial HMIs and dashboards, enterprise applications, and — further afield — embedded firmware validation and CI/CD. The work I'm built for lives where those meet: one accountable engineer who can design the panel, write the code, and commission the result, instead of handing the problem across three teams.
