About

Danielson Systems

Danielson Systems is the independent industrial controls and systems integration practice of Preston Danielson, based in The Colony, Texas. I founded it in 2026 to take on contract work in industrial controls and integration, from a single well-scoped task to broader builds spanning electrical design, PLC and HMI programming, software, and commissioning. My experience spans more than ten years across industrial automation, energy storage, material handling, aerospace, and HVAC, on both the hardware and software sides. A background in both gives me a comprehensive understanding of how a complete system fits together.

I've programmed PLC-based controls for automated sorting systems running at UPS Worldport and major US airports, and designed the panels and HMIs behind them, at Siemens Logistics. I've written safety-rated interlock logic for a robotic aircraft-manufacturing cell under a Boeing R&D contract, built containerized software for aerospace tracking systems at Raytheon, and validated commercial HVAC firmware on embedded targets at Lennox. I've also built environmental controls and remote-monitoring HMIs for Battery Energy Storage Systems at Sabre Industries, along with enterprise web applications at Fenway Group.
