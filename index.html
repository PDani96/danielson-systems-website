Danielson Systems

Industrial Controls & Systems Integration

Danielson Systems is the independent controls and systems integration practice of Preston Danielson, based in The Colony, Texas. As a single engineer experienced on both the hardware and software sides of industrial controls, I take on scoped pieces of a project, fill gaps on a team, or handle the integration work between hardware and software. My experience spans more than ten years across industrial automation, energy storage, material handling, aerospace, and HVAC.

Services

  • Industrial Controls & Integration

    PLC and HMI programming, electrical and panel design, and protocol integration across drives, sensors, and safety devices.

  • Industrial Software

    Custom HMIs, dashboards, real-time monitoring, and OT-to-IT data pipelines.

  • System Modernization & Upgrades

    Legacy HMI and PLC migrations and retrofits that preserve operational continuity.

  • Commissioning & Field Services

    On-site commissioning, FAT/SAT, troubleshooting, and cutover support.
