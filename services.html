Services

Contract work across industrial controls and integration: electrical design, programming, software, modernization, and commissioning. An engagement can be a single well-scoped task or cover several of the areas below. I'm glad to subcontract a defined piece or plug into an existing team.

  • Industrial Controls & Integration

    PLC programming, HMI development, and the electrical design that ties a control system together. Experience integrating drives, sensors, safety devices, and building-management systems into working installations.

    • PLC & HMI programming: Schneider Modicon, Allen-Bradley / FactoryTalk
    • Electrical design & panel layout: AutoCAD Electrical, EPLAN; UL508A / NEC familiarity
    • Industrial protocols: Ethernet/IP, Modbus TCP/RTU, CAN, serial / field-bus
    • BMS, sensor, and device integration: BACnet, Modbus

  • Industrial Software

    Software built for the plant floor and the equipment behind it: the layer between control hardware and the people and systems that need its data.

    • Custom industrial HMIs and dashboards
    • Real-time monitoring and OT-to-IT data pipelines
    • Edge gateway software
    • Engineering workflow automation

  • System Modernization & Upgrades

    Bringing aging control systems forward without disrupting production. Migrations and retrofits planned around operational continuity, not rip-and-replace.

    • Legacy HMI and PLC platform migrations
    • Language migrations (e.g., Visual Basic to Python)
    • Control system retrofits that preserve operational continuity

  • Commissioning & Field Services

    Getting systems running on-site and keeping them running. Startup, acceptance testing, and hands-on troubleshooting in the field.

    • On-site commissioning and startup
    • Factory and site acceptance testing (FAT / SAT)
    • Troubleshooting, field support, and cutover during migrations
